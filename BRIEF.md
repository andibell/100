# Personal Website Brief

## 1. Site purpose

Present Andi Bell as a product and experience strategist, communicate her point of view and range, and help prospective clients understand how to engage her.

## 2. Audience

Prospective clients seeking product or experience strategy, product design, innovation, research, facilitation, or related work.

## 3. Experience promise

Offer strategic clarity with creative warmth: a polished, editorial experience that feels credible and senior without feeling corporate, clinical, cold, or self-important. Keep the balance approximately 70% professional and 30% personal.

## 4. Content principles

- Preserve Andi's direct, pragmatic, human-centered voice; avoid generic marketing, UX, consulting, or AI language.
- Center her point of view: “Great products solve real problems. Anything else is just noise.”
- Show her range from high-level strategy to hands-on research, workshops, prototyping, design, facilitation, and pitching.
- Convey experience spanning startups through large-scale enterprise transformation without adding unsupported specifics.
- Include personal details selectively: painting, stained glass, textiles, printmaking, plants, dogs, and Houston, Texas.
- Never invent clients, projects, outcomes, metrics, credentials, testimonials, or other biographical facts. Mark missing content clearly as a placeholder.

## 5. Visual principles

- Aim for the sensibility of an independent creative studio or art-directed editorial publication, not a typical UX portfolio or SaaS site.
- Use warm cream, deep espresso or warm near-black, and one restrained joyful warm accent; botanical green is optional.
- Prioritize expressive editorial typography, clear hierarchy, generous whitespace, thoughtful composition, subtle asymmetry, and thin structural rules.
- Keep color restrained; make warmth, joy, and personality purposeful rather than decorative.

## 6. Interaction principles

- Keep interactions purposeful, intuitive, and restrained.
- Use subtle micro-interactions only when they clarify feedback or add a considered moment of personality.
- Avoid excessive animation and ensure motion does not obstruct content or interaction.

## 7. Accessibility principles

- Use semantic structure, meaningful link text, and a logical heading hierarchy.
- Ensure keyboard access, visible focus, sufficient color contrast, and clear interaction states.
- Provide appropriate text alternatives for meaningful images; treat purely decorative imagery as decorative.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences and do not rely on color alone to convey meaning.

## 8. MVP scope

- A responsive personal site that introduces Andi, communicates her point of view and capabilities, and provides a clear way for prospective clients to make contact.
- Include professional and personal context in balance.
- Include project or career details only when supplied and verified; otherwise use clearly labeled placeholders.
- Keep implementation simple and maintainable, favoring semantic HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript.
- No invented case studies, unsupported claims, or functionality beyond what is needed for this first version.

## 9. Explicit design anti-patterns

- Purple gradients, glassmorphism, glowing effects, or sterile pure-white presentation.
- Excessive pill-shaped UI, rounded-card layouts, containers, or unnecessary icons.
- Generic “creative technologist” visuals, corporate or clinical styling, or a self-important tone.
- Excessive animation, decoration without purpose, or overly feminine, overly playful, harsh, or excessively muted styling.
- Fake metrics, fabricated proof, buzzword-heavy copy, and other unsupported claims.
