01

Opportunity · Research · Product strategy

Purina | Accessible Expertise

Purina was exploring a pet-care support concept combining AI, expert content, self-service resources, and human guidance.

As Product & Experience Strategist / XD Lead, I connected consumer needs to the concept through research, concept testing, and interactive prototypes across three levels of support. The findings informed a Minimum Lovable Product, prioritized features, pilot strategy, roadmap, and executive business case.

Outcome Leadership had a research-backed foundation for evaluating a pilot before committing to development.

90% of research respondents reacted positively to the concept.

02

Enterprise product · Data experience

Nespresso | Sales Reporting Ecosystem

Nespresso needed a more coherent, scalable reporting experience aligned with how the business worked.

As Engagement Lead and Product & Experience Strategist, I guided research and product visioning, shaping role-based information architecture and dashboard experiences around business users’ decisions. Iterative validation, alignment around governed KPIs, and a business-driven roadmap helped establish a modular product foundation.

Outcome Reporting moved toward a single source of truth, with greater consistency around sales metrics and definitions.

12+ stakeholder interviews surfaced reporting pain points and unmet needs.

03

Operations · Decision support

Energy | Pump-by-Priority

Lease operators spent approximately 1–2 hours reviewing multiple systems to build a daily schedule, contributing to inconsistent decisions about which wells needed attention.

As XD Lead, I shaped product strategy, user experience, high-fidelity Figma experience, and executive narrative for the pump-prioritization MVP. The solution connected a prioritized daily work plan and field activity documentation with managers’ and Operations Center personnel’s ability to monitor work and update priorities.

Outcome Field work could be prioritized faster and more consistently, with operational decisions captured systematically.

Documented outcomes of the broader solution included $35M in estimated annual value, 40,000 additional barrels, and a 10–15% reduction in Lease Operating Expenses.
